Quiz Master — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

What This App Does

Quiz Master lets you register an account, browse quizzes, take them, and review your results and quiz history. An account is required to use the app.

Data We Collect

DataWhyHow long
Name, username, email, phone, address Account registration and profile Until you delete your account
Password Stored as a hash, never in plain text; used to authenticate you Until you delete your account
Quiz answers and scores To show your results and quiz history Until you delete your account
Session token Keeps you logged in between app launches; stored on-device only, in the OS's secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore) Cleared on logout or token expiry

We do not collect device identifiers, precise location, or advertising identifiers, and the app does not use analytics or ad SDKs.

How Your Data Is Used

Your account and quiz data is stored on our own servers to run the app's core features — authentication, quiz delivery, scoring, and history. We do not sell your data or share it with third parties for marketing.

Data Storage

  • Account and quiz data is stored in our database until you delete your account.
  • Your session token is stored only on your device, not on our servers.
  • We do not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Account Deletion

To request deletion of your account and associated data, contact us at the address below.

Children's Privacy

Quiz Master does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

Contact
